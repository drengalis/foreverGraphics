Minimap button release for **Forever Graphics**.

### Added
- **Minimap button.** Click it to open or close the settings panel. Drag it to move it around the edge of the minimap; its position is saved.
- **Show or hide the button.** Use the new **Show minimap button** checkbox at the bottom of the panel, or type `/fgfx minimap`. The setting is saved between sessions.

### Install
Download **ForeverGraphics-v1.2.0.zip** and extract it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`, replacing the existing `ForeverGraphics` folder.
